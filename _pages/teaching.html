Teaching and community-building have become recurring parts of my scientific work, from workshops and lectures to helping create spaces where students can participate despite geographic and time-zone barriers.

How to Grow (Almost) Anything

I joined How to Grow (Almost) Anything (HTGAA) as a committed listener in 2022 and have served as a Global TA since then.

Because the timing of the main HTGAA lectures and recitations can be difficult for students based in Asia and Oceania, I helped organize the HTGAA Asia & Oceania Review Sessions in 2024 together with Georg Tremmel and Elaine Regina.

The sessions were a self-organized response to the challenging lecture times of the main course, providing lecture and homework reviews at more accessible times for students across the region.

The experience has shaped how I think about scientific communities: making knowledge available is important, but so is making participation practically accessible.

De novo protein design at VISTEC

From 2024 to 2026, I gave lectures at VISTEC on de novo protein design, beginning with its relationship to the inverse problem of protein structure prediction and then introducing emerging computational approaches for designing new-to-nature proteins.

BioXCepTion

My involvement with BioXCepTion began as a participant in a molecular docking workshop at KVIS organized by Bundit Boonyarit. I subsequently joined the team formed around the initiative and helped teach a later workshop at the school.

Get Rich with SynBio

Get Rich with SynBio (รวยด้วย SynBio) was a Thai-language podcast project about synthetic biology.

I spearheaded the project, coordinated meetings, and developed its visual materials in collaboration with Pakpoom Subsoontorn, Cholpisit Kiattesewee, and Nattawat Leelahakorn. Much of my graphic work for the project was produced during 2021–2022.

Thai SynBio Challenge 2026

I am part of the organizing team for Thai SynBio Challenge 2026, an initiative aimed at strengthening the emerging synthetic biology community in Thailand.
